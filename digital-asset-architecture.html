The Institutional Dossier

Automated tools made tokenomics and RWA structures cheap to generate, but they all break at the same point when liquidity turns. Standard risk tools miss it: they assume positions fail independently, while shared collateral and liquidity make them fail together, in reflexive loops. Allocators have seen the clean template and know the cascade it hides. Capital follows the structure that survives the correlated drawdown.

A founder speaks in vision and code, a committee speaks in boundaries and limits. That translation gap is where weeks of diligence disappear.

THE WORK

We map the architecture (supply schedules, collateralisation, redemption logic, reserve and over-collateralisation economics) to the standard an allocator's auditor applies, then we attack it. Liquidity, leverage, collateral and incentives are modelled as a coupled dynamical system, so second-order effects surface before they are realised: de-peg spirals, reflexive collateral revaluation, contagion across shared dependencies. Every guarantee is specified, parameterised, and traced to an explicit, falsifiable assumption, pressure-tested against the conditions under which it must hold rather than the scenario that flatters it. Where the structure needs to change to survive that test, we say so and we specify precisely what has to change. Where the structure is presented to investors, we review the quantitative claims made in those materials against what the architecture actually supports, and identify the ones that will not survive technical scrutiny. We do not write the materials.

WHAT YOU GET

One document: model specification, parameter justification, stress results, and known limitations, written to the standard a Tier-1 quant team expects, precise, sourced, free of promotional language. The questions they would raise across weeks of back-and-forth are anticipated, answered and evidenced up front. The room shifts from "can we even verify this?" to "when do we proceed?"

SCOPE

Three to six weeks, fixed fee, agreed before work begins. Timed to your raise, never shortened for it. Delivered and signed by the principal.

NOT INCLUDED

Legal, securities or tax advice · drafting or filing regulatory documentation · smart contract audit or code security review · introductions to investors or any role in the raise itself · authoring investor materials, narrative or positioning work · implementation of protocol changes · any additional structure, chain or token beyond the one named in the engagement letter.

We are technical experts, never placement agents or brokers. We are paid for the work, never for the outcome of your round.

The Diligence Matrix

Technical diligence is a known interrogation. Here's what a Tier-1 quant raises and where the dossier already answers it.

THEIR QUANT ASKS
THE DOSSIER ANSWERS
"What happens to the reserve if the main collateral drops 60% in a correlated sell-off?"
Mapped. The coverage path under correlated drawdown, and the price at which backing falls below redemption obligations. § Reserve Stress
"Where does redemption actually break?"
Quantified. The trigger conditions and the threshold where redemptions outrun liquidity, venue by venue. § Failure Path
"What happens at the next unlock?"
Sized. Scheduled unlocks and emissions against the depth that actually exists where the token trades. § Supply Path
"Which assumption only holds in a bull market?"
Isolated. Every guarantee traced to a falsifiable assumption, with the ones that depend on rising prices flagged explicitly. § Assumption Ledger
"Do positions fail independently, or together?"
Modelled. Liquidity, leverage and collateral simulated as one system, including collateral re-lent through other protocols. The second-order cascade is shown, not assumed away. § Systemic Map
"Does this survive a regulator, or only a market stress test?"
Tested. Reserve, redemption and recovery mechanics checked against the rules that apply to the token (in the EU, MiCA for stablecoins), by someone who ran model validation at an ESMA-supervised rating agency. § Regulatory Fit
"Is this stress-tested, or is it a pitch?"
Independent. Written by a reviewer who did not design the structure. It documents where the model breaks, not what makes it easiest to sell. § Methodology

The questions don't change. The only variable is whether you've answered them before the meeting, or you're improvising in it.

Fractional Quant Leadership

Fractional Head of Risk

Engineers ship risk-bearing architecture, but no senior quant outside the build team is checking the math. That gap is invisible in a bull market. It surfaces when the model fails under stress, in public, with your capital and reputation attached.

THE WORK

The mandate covers the economics as well as the models. Most tokens fail institutionally for a structural reason rather than a technical one: there is no budget line an allocator can buy them from. We design the instrument so that there is, mapping the token's economics onto an existing institutional mandate, compliance requirement or reporting obligation, so the purchase decision moves from a speculative allocation nobody can approve to a line someone already owns. Emission and distribution schedules, incentive design and reserve policy are then set against what those holders actually need, not against what the market is rewarding this quarter.

WHAT YOU GET

Your model stops being the weakest part of the room. When a board or allocator asks who is checking the math, the answer is a name and a PhD, not the founder marking their own homework, without the cost or commitment of a permanent C-suite hire.

On the economics: an instrument specified against a defined institutional holder, the mandate, obligation or budget line it is bought from, the constraints that holder is subject to, and the emission, distribution and reserve policy that has to hold for it to remain acquirable. Documented well enough that your treasury, your counsel and the buyer's own committee are reading the same specification.

SCOPE

Minimum six-month term. A capped number of days per month, agreed in advance and invoiced monthly in advance. The seat is advisory and non-executive: we design and own the standard the models and the economics are held to. Implementation, execution and market operations remain with your team.

Exclusive of any validation or diligence mandate: while we hold a fractional seat we do not act as independent validator for that entity or its counterparties, and we disclose the seat to anyone who asks.

NOT INCLUDED

Treasury management, trading or execution of any kind · market-making, listing, or exchange relationships · smart contract or security review · advice to your investors or token holders on whether to buy, hold or sell · legal, tax or regulatory filings · authoring investor materials · recruitment beyond specifying the quantitative function.
