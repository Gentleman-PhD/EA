The Founder

Mauricio Gonzalez, PhD

Founder & Principal

> LinkedIn

Mauricio Gonzalez, PhD is the Founder and Principal of Euler Advisory. I hold a PhD in Applied Mathematics, jointly managed by CNRS and ENS under a European Research Council (ERC) Starting Grant project, with doctoral work rooted in dynamical systems and the modelling of large physical systems, including the French power grid. That training is not incidental to the firm: the same reliability and cascade methods that govern an electricity network are what Euler now turns on composable digital asset exposure.

I spent over four years as Head of Model Validation Europe at a credit and ESG rating agency subject to ESMA supervision, where I oversaw the full suite of risk models governed by that regime and was in frequent, direct interaction with financial supervisors. That seat, independently challenging models that carry regulatory and capital market consequences, is the discipline Euler Advisory is built around, and the reason the separation wall is treated as non-negotiable rather than a marketing line.

Prior exchange-side experience at Bitget means I know how digital asset models are built and sold, which is precisely why I can challenge them. It is what lets me test a structure the way a buyer will, and design one an institution can actually hold.

Our Standard

Uncompromising Independence

We enforce a strict separation of duties to preserve institutional trust. We test a structure or we build it, never both. And we act for one side of any transaction. Every engagement opens with a documented conflict check before any work begins.

Quantitative Rigor

We go beyond surface analytics and vendor tools. Our principal-led methodology deconstructs every system to its mathematical core: testing edge cases, applying extreme value theory and fat-tailed distributions, and setting explicit tail-risk limits for the realities of market complexity.

Board-Ready Transparency

Our deliverables are comprehensive, board-ready quantitative dossiers. Every parameter, proof, and limitation is documented with academic rigor and full transparency: empowering risk committees, allocators, and regulators to make informed, confident decisions.
