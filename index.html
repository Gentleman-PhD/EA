The economic layer: tokenomics, emissions and unlocks, reserves and redemption, collateral and liquidation logic, liquidity under stress, and the models behind them. We don't write or audit smart contracts. When an engagement needs code review, we work alongside a security partner.

An audit asks whether the code does what it says. We ask whether what it says survives a market that turns. Terra's UST collapsed in May 2022 without a code exploit: the design failed.

Every engagement is led and delivered by the founder, Mauricio Gonzalez: PhD in applied mathematics, former Head of Model Validation at an ESMA-supervised credit and ESG rating agency. Any additional specialist is named to you before seeing your data.

Two rules. We act for one side of any transaction: if we work for an issuer on a raise, we won't review that deal for an allocator. And we never independently review a structure we designed.

Reviews have a fixed scope and a fixed fee, typically three to six weeks from data access. Token design and fractional roles are retainers with a minimum term and a set number of days per month. Both are agreed after a confidential scoping call.
