May 2026 Workshop

Can Crypto Be Modelled? Microstructure, On-Chain Behaviour, and Trading Reality

ETH Zürich · FinsureTech Hub · Switzerland · Zürich

Lecturer for a technical workshop detailing the quantitative realities of digital asset markets. We cover market microstructure, specifically order book fragmentation across CEX and DEX venues and volatility regime shifts. We also explore on-chain intelligence methodologies, including network forensics, behavioural modelling, and anomaly detection, alongside systematic algorithmic execution in 24/7 high-volatility environments. Finally, the session addresses regulatory compliance and risk management, highlighting why Gaussian TradFi models fail in crypto and how to optimise portfolios under strict institutional constraints.
