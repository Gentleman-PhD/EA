Independent Methodology Validation

Credit Ratings · ESG Ratings · Hybrid Methodologies

Rating methodologies are held to the same standard by their supervisors, whatever they measure: rigorous, systematic, continuously applied, and validated by someone who did not build them. Under the CRA Regulation, methodologies must be subject to validation based on historical experience including back-testing, and reviewed periodically by a function independent of the business lines. Under Regulation (EU) 2024/3005, methodology documentation forms part of the ESMA authorisation file for ESG rating providers.

Your internal review function reports into the house that built the methodology. The consultant who helped design it cannot attest to it. Independence on the org chart is not independence in the file.

THE INTERSECTION

Environmental and transition factors are no longer adjacent to credit. They sit inside the methodology, moving obligor PD, collateral values and rating actions that must be disclosed when material. Validating that requires both disciplines in the same reviewer: someone who can back-test a transition matrix and interrogate a materiality weighting. We were built at that intersection, running validation across credit and ESG books inside a single ESMA-supervised agency.

THE WORK

We reconstruct the methodology from first principles, independently of your documentation. Every rating assumption is stated, traced, and tested against the conditions under which it must hold, not the ones that flatter it.

  • For credit: discriminatory power, calibration stability, transition-matrix consistency, and override frequency against outcome.
  • For ESG and hybrid frameworks: weighting derivation, materiality logic, and the sensitivity of the output to parameters no one has revisited since inception.

Throughout reproducibility, data lineage end to end, and the independence of the rating function assessed against the standard a supervisor applies.

WHAT YOU GET

One signed, dated opinion, addressed to your board and drafted to be filed: what was tested, what held, what did not, and a remediation list ranked by supervisory risk. The items that will be challenged first, named first. Model limits stated, not assumed away. When your board asks who challenged the methodology, the answer is a name, a PhD, and four years running that function inside an ESMA-supervised credit and ESG rating agency.

SCOPE

Four weeks from kickoff, fixed fee, agreed before work begins. Delivered and signed by the principal: no juniors, no subcontractors, no team you meet after signature. Available as a one-off validation or as a standing annual mandate covering the periodic methodology review.

NOT INCLUDED

Writing or rewriting the methodology · drafting or submitting the application · legal advice or regulatory representation · correspondence with the supervisor on your behalf · data engineering, tooling or dashboards · implementation of the remediation list · ongoing support after delivery · any methodology line or rating product beyond the one named in the engagement letter.

What the methodology document says / what the validation finds

Every methodology clears its own internal review. Here is the gap between what the documentation asserts and what independent testing establishes.

The Document Says
The Validation Finds
"Methodology reviewed annually."
Reviewed, not challenged. Reviewed by a function that reports into the house that built it. Independence on the org chart is not independence in the file.
"Weightings reflect materiality."
Unsourced. The weightings are stable and defensible in presentation; the derivation is absent, and no sensitivity analysis shows what changes if they move.
"Back-tested against historical outcomes."
Partially. Tested where data was convenient. The segments with the thinnest history carry the highest rating impact.
"Consistent application across the portfolio."
Analyst-dependent. The same inputs produce different outputs across teams. The methodology permits discretion it does not document.
"Qualitative overlay applied by senior committee."
Unbounded. The overlay can move a rating by more than the quantitative model that precedes it, with no stated limit and no audit trail.
"Data inputs are traceable."
Traceable to the vendor, not to the source. The lineage stops at the point where it would have to be reconstructed.
"Overrides are documented and exceptional."
Systematic. The override direction is consistent and the frequency is not exceptional. The committee is running a second, undocumented methodology on top of the first.

Reserve & Liquidity Model Validation

For token issuers and crypto-asset service providers under MiCA

MiCA requires issuers of asset-referenced tokens to conduct regular stress testing against severe but plausible financial and non-financial scenarios. Competent authorities must then require own funds between 20% and 40% higher where the outcome warrants it.

MiCA does not require that model to be validated externally. It does make it the model that sets your capital requirement, built in-house, tested in-house, and submitted to a supervisor who has never supervised you before.

References: Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 and associated EBA technical standards and guidelines. Provided for context; no endorsement by any authority is implied.

THE WORK

We reconstruct the reserve and redemption architecture from first principles and model collateral, liquidity, leverage and redemption logic as a coupled system rather than as independent positions, so second-order effects surface before they are realised: de-peg spirals, reflexive collateral revaluation, and contagion across shared liquidity venues. We test the stress scenarios against the EBA common reference parameters and against correlated conditions the internal programme assumed away, and we establish where the reserve stops covering redemption obligations under a path, not a point.

WHAT YOU GET

An independent read of the model that determines your own funds requirement: where it holds, where it breaks, the trigger conditions, and the distance between the two, documented to a standard a supervisor can test rather than accept. Written before the question is asked, not in response to it.

SCOPE

Four to six weeks, fixed fee, agreed before work begins.

NOT INCLUDED

Building, calibrating or rewriting the reserve or liquidity model · drafting, reviewing or submitting the white paper or authorisation file · legal, securities or regulatory advice · correspondence or meetings with the competent authority on your behalf · smart contract audit or code security review · custody, treasury or reserve management advice · selection of reserve assets or counterparties · implementation of the remediation list · ongoing monitoring after delivery · any additional token, chain or entity beyond the one named in the engagement letter.

We do not build the model we are hired to challenge. If you need the reserve model built, we will tell you who does that, and we will not be the ones who validate it afterwards.

Standing Mandate: the independent seat

Boards and risk committees increasingly need a quantitative voice that is not on the payroll of the function it oversees. Most institutions cannot find one: the people qualified to challenge a model at this level are almost all employed by someone with an interest in the answer.

THE WORK

A retained independent expert seat on your risk, audit or model governance committee. Standing review of new and revised methodologies before they reach the board. Written challenge on the assumptions that carry regulatory consequence, delivered ahead of the meeting rather than raised in it. Continuous availability to the committee chair between sessions.

WHAT YOU GET

A named, independent, PhD-level quantitative challenge function on your governance record, without building and carrying one internally, and without the conflict that comes from asking your own team to mark its own work. We hold the standard, you keep the decision. Retained annually, invoiced quarterly, with a stated minimum commitment and an agreed cap on committee hours.

SCOPE

Minimum twelve-month term, renewable. A defined number of committee cycles per year with a stated volume of methodology reviews, and a capped number of days per quarter agreed in advance. Invoiced quarterly in advance. Written challenge delivered a defined number of working days before each meeting.

The seat is advisory and non-executive: we hold the standard, the committee holds the decision. Exclusive of any validation mandate, while the seat is held we do not act as independent validator for that entity, and the seat is disclosed to anyone who asks.

NOT INCLUDED

Any validation opinion while the seat is held · drafting or reviewing regulatory submissions · legal or compliance advice · management responsibility for the review function · informal reads outside the agreed committee cycle · representation before a supervisor.
