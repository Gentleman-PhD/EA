The Stress-Test: break it before you approve it

The asymmetry in digital-asset regulation is that the risk model you are evaluating was built by the institution seeking your approval. Smart contracts executing flawlessly do not prevent economic collapse. Real supervision means dismantling the architecture to find the exact threshold where their assumptions fail, whether the target is a stablecoin issuer or a tokenized public asset.

THE WORK

We reconstruct the regulated entity's economic and risk logic from first principles, separate the assumptions that hold in any market from the ones that only hold in a rising one, and stress the structure against correlated panic. Collateral, liquidity, and redemption logic are modelled as a coupled system.

WHAT YOU GET

An independent, evidence-based read written for your supervisory committee: the exact failure point, the trigger conditions, and the true distance to ruin. You regulate the real risk, not the modelled one.

The Contagion Check: does failure spread?

Issuer reserves and liquidity buffers are almost always modelled in isolation. When market stress outpaces available liquidity, the failure spreads across shared venues. Day-one compliance tells you nothing about whether the economics survive a correlated shock.

THE WORK

We project the full systemic lifecycle: redemption pressure, shared liquidity constraints, and fiat-bridge bottlenecks simulated as a coupled system over time. We strip out speculative growth assumptions and locate the precise point where the economy ceases to be self-sustaining.

WHAT YOU GET

The exact horizon where the structure fails under stress, stated plainly enough for your policy committee to challenge, so you know whether you are approving a resilient architecture or waving through the next systemic failure.

What the filing claims / what the stress-test finds

Every applicant clears its own first look. Here is the gap between what the prospectus claims and what the architecture actually does under stress.

The Filing Claims
The Stress-Test Finds
"Fully reserved 1-to-1 fiat backing."
Bottlenecked. The fiat is there; the redemption bridges fail under mass withdrawal pressure.
"Audited, battle-tested smart contracts."
Code-safe, systemically fragile. The contracts execute flawlessly; the economic design collapses under correlated market panic.
"Over-collateralised at 150%."
Contagion-prone. The collateral holds at today's prices; it de-pegs in lockstep with the broader market during a liquidity crunch.
"Ring-fenced jurisdictional risk."
Cross-border spread. Shared liquidity venues ensure localised failure transmits instantly across the regulatory perimeter.
"Stress-tested internally by the issuer."
Modelled in a vacuum. Tested against single shocks, not cascading contagion. The reflexive feedback loop was assumed away.

The filing is built to clear your first look. The stress-test is built to survive your worst case. You regulate the second one.
