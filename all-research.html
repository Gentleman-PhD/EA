Reflexive cascading failures in digital-asset liquidity

Crypto risk is still measured with static VaR; the reflexive feedback that drives the tail (depeg → liquidation → depeg spirals and reflexive collateral cascades) is essentially unmodelled. We apply dynamical-systems and control-theory methods to a real de-peg episode, showing why static VaR misses the loss the feedback loop creates, anchored to the EBA liquidity-stress horizons.
