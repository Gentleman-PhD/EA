Evolutionary Dynamics of International Currencies & Non-Sovereign Assets

We supply a stochastic evolutionary model to compute the hazard rate of global currency regime transitions. By tracking decentralised agent coordination across UoA, MoE, and SoV functions, the framework maps metastable shifts between the dollar-centric equilibrium, sovereign challengers, and non-sovereign assets like gold and Bitcoin.

Option Pricing Across Closed Trading Windows

As equities transition toward continuous trading, a structural mismatch emerges with listed options restricted to regular hours, altering variance accumulation and intraday risk premiums. Lacking historical equity data, we construct an empirical proxy using IBIT, Deribit, and Bitcoin to model this novel market volatility dynamic.

Factorial Strategy Based on the Cross Section of Crypto Returns

We explore statistical factors in the cross-section of cryptocurrency returns to construct and backtest reliable portfolio strategies. By incorporating crypto-native variables like on-chain metrics and continuous trading dynamics, our rigorous framework isolates trustworthy predictive signals within the emerging digital factor zoo.

Execution Cost and Margin Allocation for Crypto Trading Agents

Autonomous agents on perpetual markets face two unmeasured threats to their economic viability. First, true lifetime position costs, combining execution slippage and continuous funding, can erase theoretical profits. Second, independent agents sharing collateral unknowingly concentrate exposure through hidden correlations, risking unexpected account liquidation.
